How this works

Category outlays scale with their multiplier (baseline ≈ 20.5% of GDP in total). Deviations carry real effects: more Income Security lifts demand and cuts homelessness; cutting Medicaid raises overdoses. Receipts come from your tax Acts (VAT, tariffs with Laffer saturation, church tax, LVT…). Interest grows automatically with debt × the effective rate.
