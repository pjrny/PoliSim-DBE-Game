Methodology & honesty box

  • Heuristic drivers, not RL. Each persona is a fixed objective function (welfare / nation / party / world / dark weights) that scores the engine’s own candidate moves each turn and takes the best one above threshold. Reasons quoted in the traces are the driver’s own scoring notes.
  • Mechanical preview channels only. Move scoring uses the engine’s documented preview functions (policy/budget channel deltas, group drifts). No hidden dynamics were probed; nothing here validates long-lag or stochastic effects beyond what the engine computes.
  • Deterministic seeds. Seeds s0…s9, engine FNV-1a/xorshift PRNG keyed by (seed, turn, move). Same (seeds, date) ⇒ byte-identical traces.json.
  • Baselines & actuals. “vs baseline” = the scenario’s deterministic no-op driver (pass every turn). “vs ACTUAL” exists only where a scripted real-world track exists (realign2016 dual-track; gfc2008/dbe2030 show baseline deltas only).
  • World chart is dual-scale. Dataset nations are normalized cross-country composites (World Bank/IMF); the simulated USA rides the game’s own S scale, ≈19 pts hotter. Both axes drawn — the gap is the point.
  • Counterfactual, not forecast. “This agent enacted that rule and this happened” is a statement about the model’s mechanics, not about the real world.
